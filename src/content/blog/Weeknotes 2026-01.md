I’ve been seeing this type of post format for a while in various blogs I’ve explored the past few years, and while I initially tried to make my own spin on it with [#8to](/tags/8to/), it feels less sustainable to me in the short term and I feel some sort of pressure to “conform” to the self-inflicted structure I placed before when [[/blog/random-list-of-things-to-share/|I tried starting this a few months back]].

So for now, I’ll just try to keep this up for as long as I can so I can say to myself, “I update my website every week.” hehe

***

Meta commentary of the weeknotes (before I start actually writing about the things that happened this week): I’ll try to just split things up with `<hr>` tags so at least the chunks are somewhat organized + I wouldn’t need to have a header for each and every section. All for lessening the pressure to “stick to” one type of format.

Will also try to remind myself to write casually and not just force myself to go full English all the time. It’s some subconscious thing I have considering I do want my content to be accessible to more people, but at the same time… I’d like for there to be more Filipino content in the web, not just in Facebook / Twitter / Instagram / wherever. So this is my attempt to add to it.

I also initially read some articles on weeknotes to get myself into the idea of… well, writing them:
