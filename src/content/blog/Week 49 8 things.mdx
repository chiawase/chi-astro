1. [Blooming Love](https://mangaplus.shueisha.co.jp/titles/100245) just published its last chapter early this week and I just want to say, I’m so happy that a manga about a more realistic portrayal of the coming-of-age kind of love high school to college students encounter is so refreshing to read in a sea of stories with lots of rehashed tropes. I mean, sure, this manga was also subverting tropes—the arts is the guy and the crafts is the girl, in this case—but with how their stories were laid out, it was nice to get a glimpse of this particular story. I’m happy to have witnessed it. I hope more people come to discover this hidden gem of a story 😌
