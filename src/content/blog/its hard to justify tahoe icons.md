[🔗 It’s hard to justify Tahoe icons](https://tonsky.me/blog/tahoe-icons/ "It’s hard to justify Tahoe icons")

From Niki @ tonsky.me:

> It’s delusional to think that there’s a good icon for every action if you think hard enough. There isn’t. It’s a lost battle from the start. No amount of money or “management decisions” is going to change that. The problems are 100% self-inflicted.

lol yeah. Once you try to assign an icon to _each and every_ action, you’ll find out that there are an infinite number of actions and also an infinite number of icons that could go with it. The question now is: what makes sense for the system’s / product’s context?

This was a great read shared by an officemate in our Slack team chat 😆
