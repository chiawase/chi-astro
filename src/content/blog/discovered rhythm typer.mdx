It’s so cool! It’s called [Rhythm Typer](https://rhythmtyper.net/), and you can access it in the browser! You’d need to log in using your Google account, if ever. For now it’s more of a thing you open on desktop rather than on mobile, as I initially loaded it on my phone earlier while watching the video and then just sent it to my PC to test it out.

It has everything a rhythm game usually has: the actual rhythm game, some beatmaps, a leaderboard ranking, and also a way to make your own beatmaps. Neat!

It only took a little while for me to find the beatmap shown in the video which I associate with the 3d-dancing rat 😆

This is the beatmap: [chess (slowed down & cut ver.)](https://rhythmtyper.net/beatmap/t2ugl4acjx0s)
