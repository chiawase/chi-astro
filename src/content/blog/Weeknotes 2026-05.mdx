I’m almost 1 year in my current company and only now I’m learning some Aussie slang from my officemates 😆

Some of the things I know so far:

- “under the pump” – means they’re under a lot of pressure and they’re very busy
  - initially I thought they meant they were sick or under the weather, so I said “get well soon!” but they corrected me to say they’re just busy with back-to-back meetings 😅
  - only when I was corrected did it occur to me to search what it was, and it was apparently Aussie slang :))
- “arvo” – means afternoon
  - this time around when I saw this very foreign-to-me looking word, I immediately searched what it meant. It’s slang used in Australia and New Zealand, apparently! I knew then how to respond, and also spelled out “afternoon” so I also remember what it means if I do backread our chats 😆

There’s bound to be more, maybe I can mimic [anh’s Personal Lexicon](https://anhvn.com/garden/lexicon/) and also add a page in my website for things I wanna remember the meaning of. :))
