### Still doing kendo

Since my [last post](/blog/turning-30/), I did write that kendo is fun, and now even more so as we’re learning kata[^1] and are also starting to do jigeiko[^2] with the senseis in full bougu[^3]. So far… I did nice last time—finally ramped up the courage to face our head sensei first and just take it on—but I know I have so much more I need to fix with my posture and how I do my strikes. It is tiring… but I will just keep on doing it, as it is nice to see the improvements I have, albeit small sometimes as I haven’t gotten back to doing suburi every other day 😅

It helps that my batch is close enough that we encourage each other to do better, and we also have our own invites who are technically our kouhai who started in June as well.

I’m gonna try to get 1st kyu on October 2026, which… is gonna be a challenge for me, as I am gonna be coming from another overseas trip then 😅 Initially I didn’t really _plan_ to progress this quickly for kendo but I guess if the opportunity is there, I might as well try it. At worst, if I do gun for 1st kyu and I don’t pass the sparring time, I guess I could get 2nd kyu?

But I’ll see. That’s in 3 months. A lot can change between now and then.

It’s been hard to keep a routine. I did get a bit disrupted due to my 10-day travel, but it’s been around a month since I got back and I haven’t gotten my old routine back, where my partner and I would find time to go to the gym, and also do some kendo suburi, and just play FFXIV.

I still have lots to explore there and also lots to work on with the classes, so that still felt like a goal or a doable task to complete.

I don’t have too much of a drive for other recent games, and I haven’t even played games as much on my PC (mostly since we do have a PS5 Pro and even a Steam Deck, if I really wanted to play something, I’d go to the TV instead of in front of my PC and at my desk).

I do want to write a separate post for this when I get to it, maybe in the next few days (and I say this because that’s my mental deadline for it 😆) and I’ll collate some more thoughts on that and repost on LinkedIn as well lol (gotta get them views 😛)

I don’t think I’ll step back forever.
