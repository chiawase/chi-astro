***

Random share: earlier today I also attended my second weekday keiko session since the club moved to a new spot. I didn’t feel the nerves as much as before, kasi this time around I was focusing on really just learning from everyone else around me, kung ano ba mang learning ma-iimpart sakin as one of the most beginner people in that room 🙈

After the keiko session and I went to bow and give thanks to our head sensei, he told me I had good kamae ;u; aaaaa tapos yung mga senpai ko napa hirit pa hAHA shet, happy lang ako na at least kita or recognized yung progress ko? I remember my first ever weekday keiko session, nung natapat ako kay Wada-sensei nun for the rounds, ang dami niyang sinita sa kamae ko hahaha (pero kasi <abbr title="to be fair">tbf</abbr> ang dami ko nga rin namang mali nun XD) So hearing this from him was nice, and I’ll hold onto these words as fuel and guidance ko as I continue practicing :))

I didn’t think kendo would take this much of my time now when I started back in January, but the more I do it the more it feels nice to see the continuous progress? Pero ang daaaaami pa rin kailangan alamin at aralin :)) Sometimes it feels overwhelming. But for times like that, I remind myself na lang na sarili ko naman ring pace ito, with the occasional goading from batchmates that I think is also nice naman since it helps us all in a way 😆 It’s fun rin naman to do activities together, and to see everyone else also improve over time is nice too :))

***

The past few months, and since [turning 30](/blog/turning-30/) (wow naman HAHA) mas napapareflect ako sa mga tao I keep sa buhay ko. And also the acknowledgement that there will be people who come and go, and regardless of the reason, ok lang yun. Ganun talaga buhay.

Younger me would grieve longer for the connections I’d lose or drift apart from, pero now I know to give some space for it, but to not dwell on it for long. Wala, time changes us, and that sometimes means that our interests with others wouldn’t intersect as much anymore. I’d still be thankful for the people I’ve gotten to know with over the years, with all the memories and experiences I’ve gone through and the thoughts I’d have at the time, pero I know rin na posible ring I look back at some of these experiences and see it in a different light. Parang yung feeling of watching a movie you loved when you were a child, iba rin yung dating niya pag pinanood mo nang matanda ka na eh 😆 Like aside from some jokes or references that would fly over your head nung bata ka, our own experiences would also influence what we’d think about certain scenes in some movies or TV shows and such. :))

It’s still a bit awkward for me to confront it, or to sit with the feeling. But given enough time, it eventually … grows on me? Like di ko na lang mamadaliin sarili ko to “feel” or process things. Ganun naman talaga dapat ata XD
