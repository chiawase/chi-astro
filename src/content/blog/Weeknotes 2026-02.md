Another week has passed! The first full working week was tough to get back into lol. I ended up mostly doing the bulk of my work in Thursday and Friday because I was a bit too distracted with updating stuff on my website at the start of this week, as you can see in my [changelog](/changelog/) 😛 (Oh and, yeah I started maintaining a changelog for my website so I don’t spam people with posts mostly talking about the things I tinker with here, and I so I can give myself “space” to talk about other things 😆)

I still don’t know how else I want to do these weeknotes. I guess if I want to really separate some ideas or thoughts, I’ll just use `<hr>` tags or `***` in my Markdown. I think that works for me for now, as I do tend to ramble and I like seeing my thoughts laid out in paragraphs.

***

We recently finished rewatching 86 (anime) (link to dedicated anime media page pending) and man, I’m looking forward to watching the next season of this. I generally knew about the story and what kinda _happened_, but I didn’t remember the exact sequence of events and also when the reveals happened. ***

I’m going to Thailand next week! This isn’t my first time, nor is it my second, but I guess it’s technically still a new experience for me to travel somewhere and will mostly still work. I think we’ll still have a lot of time to do some more touristy things, and I’d definitely want to be there for my boyfriend so he could have a break from his startup life :))

I’m also coming to terms with the realization that I have been traveling a lot more now, and not just for work. These are technically leisure trips! I’m happy I get to do this now, as I didn’t really have that many opportunities before. I have the money to do so now, and also the time and energy, so I might as well do it while I can hehe

***

In relation to the previous section, I also want to eventually log all the trips I’ve done in my website too. Even past trips. It’ll take some effort to remember what I did though, like the time I did my first ever solo travel to Taiwan back in 2023. I don’t even remember now why I decided to do that, maybe it had something to do with having to use up some remaining Travel Fund in my airline account that was going to expire soon, but it was a nice experience that I’d like to repeat again.

I have lots of photos from that time as I initially intended to make a vlog out of it, but I never really managed to do that lol. I also have some footage when I first visited Singapore in 2024, which I also didn’t get to post all of it because by the time I got back to the Philippines again, I got eaten up by the idea that _maybe no one wants to see this anyway_ kinda thoughts. I realize now that that’s wrong, and I was just poisoned by the social media mindset to want likes and attention[^1], so now I want to reclaim a bit of that energy a bit and do my future self a favor and write stuff down.
