I found out about Micro.blog around 2021 and figured I'd keep my content there as I liked the idea of being able to surface my posts to the fediverse (wherever that was supported). At some point though, I needed to downscale some of my subscriptions, and also thought maybe I could move my website to a self-hosted option and experiment with rendering my site from a static site generator.

Most of the posts here were what was originally hosted in my former `/archives/` page, and with my [WordPress](/archives/wordpress/) posts also being housed here, I figured I'd separate them for clarity.
