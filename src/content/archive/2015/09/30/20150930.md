I do not want to see myself.
Not now.

I only thought of what I wanted to
do, and
not what might have been better for you; I
want to dissolve in the fizzle inside me
to never ever disappoint you again. I
see how my actions can affect the people around me, even
myself; why do I keep trying? Well,

not anymore.
Now I know better, now I know that I shouldn't try anymore.
