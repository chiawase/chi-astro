what happened to the days when
you could just pick yourself up from the den
that you have fallen into this morning
and you slept through it last night
this never bothered your thinking
other than, "hey, it smells bad here,
let's get out as soon as we can,"
what happened to those days?

what happened to the times when
the moon would still shine truly bright
when sometimes the stars would block your light
but you managed to wipe them away
with your beams and just say,
"hey, we can go through this together,"
and you did,
what happened to those times?

what happened to the days when
it wasn't a struggle to keep upright
the wind would make you bend a bit,
but you would stand up and rustle your leaves
some would fall down to the ground,
but you would look at them and say,
"hey, what's past is past, and it wasn't as bad,"
what happened to those days?
