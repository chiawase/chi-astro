**repetition of tasks**
**while** roused**:**
 move **in** deliberately
 make**(**careful strokes**)**
 **!(**lose your grip**)**
 suck it up
 perform**(**you > better**)**
 **if** you > limit**:**
  release**(**inside**)**
  exhale**()**
  **continue**
