Awww, NaPoWriMo's over. And I didn't get to do one poem everyday.

Oh well. It was worth a shot. At least I got back to writing! :)

After I was gone for a few days (reason: organization thing), I lost my will to write, plus I got really busy with other things.

I had a lot in mind to write about, but I just couldn't find the right words to express them.

I wish to overcome this. And it's one of my goals for each passing week.
