caught in-between
two paths --
once intertwined
now extricated;

both looking
for the same light,
both searching
throughout the night.

by day, they say
they're never the same
in the end, they create
all this disarray.

one path leads to
the unknown new,
and the other continues --
a little askew?

time will tell
what happens when
they interlock
all over again

or maybe not,
it has yet to come --
two paths untwined
in a steady recline.
