the problem about writing
your feelings: letting them ooze out
from the text you're reading
is that the hurt keeps coming back
after you read them;
instead of photographs,
you left shards of glass
and now, you're stepping
on them, one by one.

(don't do this to yourself. you don't need this right now.)
