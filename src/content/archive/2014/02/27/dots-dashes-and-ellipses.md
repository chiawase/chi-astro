what else can I say
after a dot.
after a hanging line --
after an ellipse...

thoughts that keep
eating my mind.
words at the end of my tongue --
lost in my mind...

do I still need to say
anything.
if I don't even need to speak --
what's the use of talking...

just listen
it'll be alright in the end.
I know that's bullshit --
better that white lie than nothing...

you need to stop
beating yourself up.
but what if I deserve it --
what about tomorrow...

the next day
will be the day you wake up.
doesn't matter how many hours you slept --
no one there cares anyway...
