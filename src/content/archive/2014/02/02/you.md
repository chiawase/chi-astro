you say sadness is the only thing you know
but are you willing to go past that?
do you want to know more things?
things more than sadness?

there are lots of other things beyond the mountain
this mountain that you are on
you say that you are used to this sadness
and yet you look for more
because you can still see more,
at the bottom of the mountain
you can see wonders, you are curious
as to what you may encounter
once you go down this mountain
if you go down this mountain

I assure you, there is more
more than what you know
it may be exciting, it may be scary
there are lots of things that are scary
but I'm here. I'll go with you
I'll be your guide.
I won't ever let go of your hand
if you don't let go of mine
I'll lead you to places
you've never heard of before
places you haven't dreamed of
I'll let you experience the wonders
of what's beyond this mountain
that you reside in
but only if you allow me to guide you.
