resolve:
waking up
to the sound
of the alarm
to let me know
that I should go
and call you now
to let you know
I am alright
my sleep was fine
I wonder now
about your part

decision:
to call you
only to realize
you are still
deep in slumber
now waiting
for an hour
before calling again
to wake you up
and to let you know
i am here

situation:
making sure
that you were fine
last night
apologizing
for last night
all night
letting you know
i suddenly slept
i didn't mean to

message:
I'm sorry
I didn't mean to
leave you in reality
I feel bad
I shouldn't have done that
I'm sorry
I really am--

response:
It's okay.
You needed to rest.
I figured you slept.
No need to worry.

outcome:
Another day,
another time to smile.
