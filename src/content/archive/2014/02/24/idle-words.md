the hardest part of writing:
when you hit that point when you don't know what to write about
at that exact moment
when all that comes out of your mind is just gibberish
words that don't mean anything when compiled into one sentence
one line won't mean anything anymore
they're just words
words that mean something individually
but put them together and they don't mean anything
or if ever they do, only someone as high as the sky
will understand, but no one else will
it will be something profound for them
but it doesn't really mean anything
you want to reread everything from the start
but you don't even understand what you're trying to say
what you want to say
the words take over your fingers
as they write down the words being listed down in your brain
you don't know what else to say
to people who ask what you're writing
it's just random blabbering--

what are you writing?
