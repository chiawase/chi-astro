maybe if I didn't draw only faces,
I would be proud of what I've drawn.

maybe if I didn't see the things that scarred me,
I would be proud of what I've seen.

maybe if I didn't listen to the advice you gave me,
I would be proud of what I've heard.

maybe if I didn't do the things I shouldn't have,
I would be proud of what I've done.

maybe if I didn't say all those swear words,
I would be proud of what I've said.

maybe if I didn't write more about my feelings,
I would be proud of what I've written.
