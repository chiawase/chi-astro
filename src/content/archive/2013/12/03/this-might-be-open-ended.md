can you easily tell the difference
between a lie and a sigh?

what's different about these two?
what do you think is true
or what are you more used to?

a lie may not always have a sigh
a sigh may not always be a lie
lies can have lots of sighs
sighs may mean more and more lies

but a sigh can simply mean depression
a lie can be about an impression --
it's really up to your discretion
which brings me back to my question
as to which has the more tension

does sighing mean that you're lying?
does lying mean you have to be sighing?
I don't really know if there's an underlying
message behind all this whining

but despite all of that confusion
I really can't come up to a conclusion
if this is all just an illusion

what's the difference between a lie and a sigh?
and another thing to ask is: why?

I guess I did leave this open-ended.
