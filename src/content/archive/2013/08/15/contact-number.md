The number you have dialed
is incorrect
please try again; or maybe not
you might have gotten it wrong anyway.

The number you have dialed
is busy at the moment
and every other moment
that passes in your life;

The number you have dialed
is either unattended
or out of coverage area
for you cannot reach the person

Sorry, you cannot leave a message
for this number
for you will never mean anything
to the person you are calling
