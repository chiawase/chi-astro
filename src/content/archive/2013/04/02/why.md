Welcome to the world
Hate it as you may;
You cannot escape your existence.

Are you happy? Are you sad?
Maybe, maybe not

I don't know what to feel

Should I just look up
to the sky, hoping and praying -
indescribable emotion -
let everything go,
let everything be.

So we continue our life
as it is;
doing the same things we do.

All your problems will fade away
not at once; but it will,
don't worry.

My soul is aloof
in this void we call life.
So it is told that
everyone lives in this world,
running through everything
always hoping
because we're not sure;
life manages to surprise
even the most experienced man.
