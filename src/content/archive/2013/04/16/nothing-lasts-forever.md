---
title: "Nothing lasts forever"
date: 2013-04-16T21:00:52+08:00
tags: ["poems"]
source: wordpress
originalUrl: "https://chiawase.wordpress.com/2013/04/16/nothing-lasts-forever/"

No one will care.
Nothing will exist anymore.
What you think is real may be imaginary for others.
Don't be afraid to be you.

Your words may be nonsense for others,
but not for you.

Everything is clear, when you're the one speaking.
You may not know what others will think,
but you know yours.

But thoughts can drift away.
