I can't get out. I don't know how.
How do you get out of something like this?
I need to make a decision right now
(I'm not even the one who needs to make the decision!)

You, you there.
You're really nice. I like you.
I thought it was impossible for someone like me
to get along with someone like you
and actually go together,
just us two
but no

You, you came along.
You left, and went back.
Your things are still with me;
(I never bothered to throw them away)
but now it seems like you can just
go back to the way it was
before

No
what do I do

Do I get you back
or do I go on and get out of this whack?

Do I stay
or do I go?
The move I play
will determine the direction I will go

...
I don't know.

I'm stuck between you two.
