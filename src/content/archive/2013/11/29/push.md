sometimes, all we need is a little push
to get things going, get things to flow
but that little push might be too much
for the person who feels so small
a little push might mean different to him
like changing his whole life, instead of the usual
'just try and see what happens' kind of deal
if a push is all that it takes to succeed
then pressing all the buttons in the world
will end world hunger and give us world peace
if a push was all that we needed to do things,
then pushing all the people in the world forward
would've pushed us forward in time
if a push is that powerful to affect us
then just tripping on your foot
can change your morals and ways
what if the push was all that you needed
to get on with your life

are you willing to take the next step
or do you need to be pushed to get there?
either way, both lead you to the same direction
the question is: do you really want to go there?
or would you rather stay
on solid ground?
