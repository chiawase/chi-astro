---
title: "I don't like being watched."
date: 2013-05-24T09:49:14+08:00
tags: ["break","death","gaze","move","poems","pressure","rope","stress","thoughts","watched"]
source: wordpress
---

every plan I try to maneuver
I always have to endure
the pain from the eyes
watching me, my mind
cannot take this

after some time,
I might break down and cry
from the pressure I
am feeling
please stop this
stop staring at me

You think I can take this?
I am disintegrating
slowly, I feel like dying
this very moment
Just let the rope hang

would you still watch me
as I circle around in the air
with my gaze deadlocked
at the unfinished work
at the table;

would you still stare
at my worn out body
that once used to hide
all the pressure
that was felt inside;

will I still feel the pain
of your eyes watching my every move
as I slowly float above the chair
I used to climb up to escape
this life I cannot endure
