hey there
I think
I love you
do I?

What is love for me?
What is love for you?
do I really
love you
like I think
I do?

Maybe this is just me
looking for someone to be
with and enjoy
life with
just someone to hold
in my arms
just someone to touch
in places unimaginable

maybe I just want love
or maybe I just want touch
is this actually love?
I want it now.

If you want love,
does that mean you
don't love at all?

If I want to love you,
does that I mean I don't love you
at all?

can you just please
hold my hand
ruffle my hair
let me do the same to you
let me love you.
