Before going to sleep,
remembering the feeling of being
in your arms
allowing myself to be comfortable ---
now that's wonderful.

Let me hold your hand
and just stay like this, thinking about
'us', I know this is not a dream.
Reality: that's what's here now
either way, we're both here
not moving an inch away ---
calm and collected;
everyday, I want to be like this.

Rest assured that
every morning,
you will be in my mind;
every time I close my eyes
somehow, I'll hear your voice, too.

Go with me to wherever;
Onward, we will walk together.
