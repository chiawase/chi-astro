I remember
how long I waited
for this moment:
to be part
of your universe;
to be amongst the stars
that you see every night;
to be the clouds that pass by
your house every morning;
the wind that blows
through your short hair,
the water that flows
by the river;
all of these,
I can be part of -
the wait was worth it.
