It's been a long time.
(Time is relative.)

Take it nice and slow. No rush to it.
(You hold the key to make this matter.)

Whatever happens, just take it as it is.
(Don't over think things.)

Take the time to feel the breeze.
(It never hurts to try.)

If it suddenly rains on your parade, just keep walking.
(If you can't change it, accept it. And vice versa.)

Be thankful for what you already have.
(Don't keep looking for things that aren't there.)

In the end, just do baby steps.
(No need to be hasty.)

In the end, you'll get there eventually.
