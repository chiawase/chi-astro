uncertainty is always around the corner. wherever you go, with whomever you meet, whatever time of the day -- it's always there.

uncertainty.

going on and about with your life, you're always never sure. forward or back, up or down, left or right, go or stop. you're never too sure about things.

when you think you are, you suddenly realize you aren't. whenever you think you aren't, sometimes there's that chance that you are. whatever it is, it's always uncertain.

you continue on typing the rest of your mantra, going on and about with whatever you have to do, making yourself focus on the things you deem important; but after hitting 'enter'--

-- what do you type next?

_

you stare at the blank space.

keep blinking. it keeps blinking at you.

_

what else can you add?

_
