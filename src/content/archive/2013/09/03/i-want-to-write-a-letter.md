write a letter
and give it to someone
whoever it may be
just write a letter
and put it in an envelope
for them to see
writing a letter
for a special someone
maybe
I don't really know what to write in a letter
besides what I want us to be --
no wait, you might run away
from me

to write a letter
based on what's on my mind
to write a letter
based on the things I find
interesting; to write a letter
about the night sky
to write a letter
because I want to lie
to write a letter
so I can say goodbye

a letter is what I need
to type in this phrase
letters are what make up
our sentences in our papers
the pixels in our text files
ink in our written works
a letter is what I want
to give to you
in order to let you know
what I feel;
what I see;
what I hear;
what I want to be;
what I want us to be.

The letter
that I will write later;
will I have the courage to give you
the letter?
Maybe I will,
maybe I won't
the letter
will be my release
of emotions;
the letter
of my deepest,
darkest secrets.

If you manage to stumble upon that letter, would you read it?
If you manage to read that letter, would you ask me about it?
If you manage to find me, would you want to talk to me about it?
If you manage to talk to me, would you think about it?
If you do think about it,
would you remember me?
