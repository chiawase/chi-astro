I don't know, man.

Can you explain how you are annoyed at someone?
Sometimes you can.

Sometimes you can't...?

Are you sure?

Maybe it's for some deep-rooted reason that you can't simply dig up to the surface. Or maybe it's just a shallow reason, as shallow as the waters you go through every now and then.

Maybe it's just that. Or maybe it's more than that.

Maybe it's related to a bunch of problems you've been going through. Or maybe it's not related to anything at all. Maybe it's just a by-the-moment thing. Or maybe it's a recurring event.

Maybe it means a lot of things. Maybe it means nothing at all.

But one thing's for sure: I'm annoyed at you.
