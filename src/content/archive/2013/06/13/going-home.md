tears start to fall
on top of your cover
you can feel it all
making your body ache all over

you have your feet
in buckets of water
they feel so beat
from plunging in the gutter

you continue to walk
through the muddy pathways
checking your clock,
your pattern replays

on and on, you continue
heading to where your heart is
by then, you can resume
living your life with ease

as you arrive,
you let everything go
now you feel more alive
while your dog says 'hello'

removing all the extra load
attached to your body;
now, in your humble abode
around with nobody.
