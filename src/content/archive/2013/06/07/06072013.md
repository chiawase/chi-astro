It may not be much,
but whenever I share
anything, just anything
with you --
food or drinks, maybe some wine
jokes or laughs, maybe some time --
I enjoy more of my life.

It might not be much
to get a bit of your time
and spend it with you all the while
but as long as both of us --
I'm pretty sure both of us will
be happy
with each other's company.

It might not be much
that I still try
to make you (sort of?) mine;
I know there's a thin line
between I'm being yours
and you being mine,
but that's fine.

It might not be much
but at least I do try;
it's better than not doing anything
and to just cry;
it's better that I'm almost there
right by your side
instead of not being there at all.
