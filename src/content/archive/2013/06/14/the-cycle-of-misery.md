---
title: "The Cycle of Misery"

I have this unending feeling of queasiness
I cannot shake it off so easily;

Every time I remember past happenings
Everything starts to shake

It's like I do not have my footing anymore,
It's like there's an earthquake

Let me out of this never-ending misery, please
Let this queasiness disappear

When I think of you, even if I have no ill thoughts
When this happens, the feeling comes back

Go away, leave me be, let me rest, just for once
Go on and move along this road

You should continue on the path, I'll be okay
You're better off without me anyway

What am I saying? You wouldn't even care
What I think is nothing for you

The queasiness takes over my body, please just let
The misery end, just go away from my mind

A soft cry is heard in the distance;
A woman, left alone.
