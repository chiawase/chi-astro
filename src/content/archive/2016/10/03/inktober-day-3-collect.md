First day drawing at the office. Here's what I came up with!

![img_20161003_132810.jpg](https://chiawase.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/img_20161003_132810.jpg?w=1024)

 Collect, as in, I need to collect all my thoughts? Harvest them? Maybe???

I slowly want to transition just using pens for drawing/sketching, but I was a bit scared of screwing up this one, hence the pencil lines.

I could also just keep at this, since I technically like the look of just leaving pencil lines on a drawing. It feels... more real. Less clean, but more... nice for me. Haha.
