I thought hard about this. This is what I came up with.

![img_20161008_162303.jpg](https://chiawase.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/img_20161008_162303.jpg?w=1024)

 Bato... bato pick? Ewan.

*Translation: The sign says, "Buy-buy Rock-rock". The bald guy's saying, "Go choose." And I'm thinking, "It's already Day 8 of inktober. The prompt is **Rock**." Bato bato pick is also Tagalog/Filipino for the game Rock Paper Scissors.*

I didn't want to draw just one measly rock... so I ended up drawing... this.

I don't think I've ever encountered a shop in the Philippines that sells *only* pairs of rocks, though. This is just me trying to fit the situation into my joke. :P
