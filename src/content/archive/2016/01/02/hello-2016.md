It's been a while since I last posted in this blog. I haven't felt the urge to write last semester, and I also did not see the need to write things down.

But now, I sorta do.

Hello also to you, whoever's reading this. I'm not exactly the type of person who easily connects to people I just meet on the Internet. I really just don't know what to say--my awkwardness knows no bounds, apparently.

To be honest, I don’t really know what to write here. I just… feel like writing, that’s all. I like typing things out, and lately I know that I need to do that more often. I have thesis this year, which calls for me to write stuff out (so writing random posts in English _may_ actually be helpful for me to practice my grammar and writing style), and I’m also part of our org’s publication. (You should go [check it out](http://wildcard.compsat.org)! We have cool IT-related and not-IT-related stuff there. :) )

Anyway, going back to what I wanted to... say? Write? I guess I just wanted to document this idea that I have in mind.

**I want to write more**, is what I'm saying. I've said this to myself a couple of times already last year, and even the year before that. I've _always_ had a hard time committing myself to certain things that needed to be updated at a constant pace. I think it stems from the fact that I'm a person that loves starting things, but has a hard time finishing them. (Hello, group projects from way before. Hello also, to old comics and stories I attempted to do.)

But now, I want to try and challenge myself. I want to try and develop this habit of just writing stuff out (and correct my grammar along the way!) so that I find another productive thing to do. I've always loved the thought of creating things. And because I'm on my laptop almost every day, then I might as well try and keep this blog running for as long as possible.

I started this around 3 years ago, and it was another reboot of several other blogs I've tried to start, but lost interest in doing because of reasons. They never lasted a year. But since this is still around... I think I can do something about it.

And what will I do? What will I write about? Will I continue my poetry? Write some fiction? Continue making sappy personal posts?

Well, for starters, I'll do all the things I mentioned above. Or, I'll just do what I feel like doing. I see a lot of successful people who just do what they do, and they all look happy doing whatever it is that they're doing. I want to try and do what they're doing, which is to do what I want to do. (That's a lot of 'do's. I hope I actually follow through.)

There are times when I felt like, "whoa, this game is super cool! I love this and that, but I think this could be better," and I thought, hey, that would be perfect for a game review! And I got pretty excited with the thought of doing that.

There's also my poetry, which I haven't really... tried to improve on. I just kept on doing the "no rhymes, just feelings" kind of deal. That's nice... for some. But for almost all my poems? No. I don't want to look back and think, "man, I was so sappy and emotional then." I have done that countless times while looking through my past posts in this blog. You will, too, if you go through my poetry. I'm a sappy love poet.

This is getting pretty long, and a voice in my mind is telling me to go back to studying stuff for my thesis (which is sorta due in a few days because of opportunities! Hehe). I'll just leave this list here for some kind of reference on what to expect here:

- more poetry - this _was_ where I kept my musings, so I'll try to make more of those sappy love poems. Or make some other kind of non-love poems.

- my thoughts on certain games, movies, books, music videos (?) - I've been wanting to do this for a while now, so there's that.

- projects I've been wanting to try...? - Or you know, just promote the stuff I already have, like my [art blog](http://cheesysketchies.tumblr.com) and this [personal art project I haven't gotten back to since God knows how long](http://noerasuresallowed.tumblr.com)!

Well, there's that. I hope I pull through with the plans I have in mind. Thanks for stopping by!
